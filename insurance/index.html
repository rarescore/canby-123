Insurance

Get ready for your visit and ask us about coverage or costs.

Bring what you have

CoverageYour insurance card
IdentificationA photo ID
Your medicinesA current list

No insurance? Call us to discuss your options.

(818) 674-4414office@canbycc.org

Bring these.

Insurance card

Bring it if you have one. If you do not, say that when you call.

Photo ID

A driver’s license or another photo ID.

Medicines

The names, and the dose if you know it. A list on paper is enough.

Ask about the cost before you come.

Call (818) 674-4414 and ask what the visit may cost, and whether we can bill your plan. The clinic can confirm whether your plan is accepted.

What to do when your health coverage changes

Call (818) 674-4414

Need coverage itself, not a clinic bill? 211, Covered California, Medi-Cal, and 988 are on the resources page.
