Your first visit

Let’s plan
your visit.

01

Tell us what you need.

Share a reason for the visit and a preferred day and time.

Use the visit form and pick a day and time.

02

If that day is full.

We'll offer another. You don't have to fill the form out again.

03

Bring the useful details.

Photo ID, a medication list, and relevant records help us keep a more accurate history.

Ask about accommodations, language help, or other access needs before the visit.

04

Can't find the suite?

Call from the parking lot. Someone will walk you to 6B.
