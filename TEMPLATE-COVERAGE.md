## Archive routes and design

- Services: six care areas, full descriptions and qualifications, consultation backdrop and care panels.
- Appointments: request, confirm, prepare, arrive; paired elevator doors and supplied scroll film.
- New patients: preparation image and individual preparation panels.
- Resources: six folder-style guides linked to relevant pages.
- Visit: address, hours, arrival guidance, neighborhood approach, external directions.
- Our clinic: listen, explain, connect content; team scene and restrained reveals.
- Volunteer: medical/non-medical pathways, role requirements, separate validated email-draft forms.
- Donate: giving areas, preset/custom amounts, monthly/one-time selection, payment-option preview and demo receipt. Live payments intentionally deferred.
- Request a visit: callback details, contact preference, patient status, availability, confirmation acknowledgment.
- Legal: accessibility, nondiscrimination, privacy, notice of privacy practices, emergency information. Unprovided approved notices remain identified as unavailable.
- Supplied Canby / Pura Vida logo used intact in shared header and footer.
- Old archive and template URLs route to updated destinations; details in README.md.

Motion is removed or simplified for reduced-motion preferences. Mobile layouts preserve the reading order and all content.
