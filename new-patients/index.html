New patients / Reseda

Your first visit.

Know what to share, what to bring, and what to ask before you leave home.

Request a visit

What to bring.

Before

Reason for your visit

Describe the main question or concern in your own words.

You do not need to diagnose yourself before calling.

Bring

ID, medications, records

Bring photo ID, an updated medication list, and relevant records when available.

Do not send sensitive medical information through an unapproved form.

Ask

Questions that matter

Write down the questions you do not want to forget.

Ask again if a word or a step is unclear.

Leave with

A visible next step

Confirm what happens next, when to follow up, and who to contact.

Before leaving, ask how results or referrals will be communicated.

Your preparation list

Bring what you have.

You do not need to arrive with every answer. Start with the information you have and the questions that matter.

During your visit

Questions worth asking.

What happens next?

Ask about your next action and when to follow up.

How will I hear about results?

Confirm how test results or referrals will be communicated.

Who should I contact?

Write down where to direct questions about the plan.
