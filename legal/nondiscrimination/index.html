Clinic information

Nondiscrimination

Care at Canby is for the person in front of us.

Request a visit

Canby Community Clinic complies with applicable Federal civil rights laws and does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national origin, age, disability, or sex. Sex includes pregnancy, sexual orientation, and gender identity.

We also do not discriminate because of religion, ancestry, marital status, or medical condition.

Help is free

If you need help to talk with us, or to read something we give you, call (818) 674-4414. We can arrange a qualified interpreter, or information in another format, at no cost to you.

If you believe we treated you unfairly

Write or call the Civil Rights Coordinator, Canby Community Clinic, 7601 Canby Ave #6B, Reseda, CA 91335, (818) 674-4414, info@canbycc.org. We will not retaliate.

You can also file a civil rights complaint with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office for Civil Rights, at ocrportal.hhs.gov or 1-800-368-1019.
